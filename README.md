## Overview

This repository contains a comprehensive collection of three educational presentations designed for video creation, self-study, and exam preparation for the AWS Certified Generative AI Developer – Professional certification. 

Each presentation features detailed slide content, beginner-friendly explanations, and practical examples that connect AWS services to real-world Generative AI applications.

## 1. Amazon Q Business

**Description:**  
A deep dive into Amazon Q Business, a fully managed generative AI assistant for enterprise workplaces. This presentation explains how Q Business connects to organizational data, answers employee questions, summarizes documents, and takes actions through configured plugins.

**Key Topics Covered:**
*   **Core Concepts:** Natural-language Q&A, content generation, and source citations.
*   **Data Sources & Connectors:** Integrating with 40+ enterprise sources (S3, SharePoint, Salesforce).
*   **Plugins & Business Actions:** Creating Jira tickets, scheduling meetings, and more.
*   **Identity & Security:** Using AWS IAM Identity Center and permission mapping.
*   **Administrative Controls:** Guardrails, blocked topics, and response styles.
*   **Q Apps:** Building reusable, purpose-built AI applications.

## 2. Amazon Quick

**Description:**  
An introduction to Amazon Quick (formerly Amazon Quick Suite), an AI-powered workspace that brings together AI agents, business research, data analytics, and workflow automation. This presentation covers how employees can use natural language to research, analyze data, and automate tasks.

**Key Topics Covered:**
*   **Core Concepts:** AI agents, Spaces, and connected data sources.
*   **Key Features:** Quick Research, Quick Sight, Quick Flows, and Quick Automate.
*   **Real-World Application:** A practical example of a Sales Performance Assistant.
*   **Security & Best Practices:** Permissions, data quality, and human oversight.

## 3. Operational Efficiency and Optimization

**Description:**  
A technical deep dive into how to optimize AWS Generative AI applications. Building an AI app is one thing; making it fast, cost-effective, reliable, and scalable is another. This presentation covers Amazon Bedrock best practices and production-ready strategies.

**Key Topics Covered:**
*   **Token Efficiency:** Managing context and using RAG to reduce costs.
*   **Model Selection:** Benchmarking and choosing the right model for the task.
*   **Resource & Throughput:** Monitoring with CloudWatch and matching capacity to demand.
*   **Caching Strategies:** Prompt caching and application-level caching.
*   **Responsiveness & Resilience:** Streaming responses, exponential backoff, and jitter.
*   **Performance:** Optimizing foundation models and compute instances.
*   **Cross-Region Inference:** How Amazon Bedrock routes traffic across eligible AWS Regions.
